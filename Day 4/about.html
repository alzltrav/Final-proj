Welcome to Ichigo no Umi

At Ichigo no Umi, every dish tells a story, a harmony between the timeless elegance of Japanese cuisine and the playful sweetness of strawberries. Our name, which means “Strawberry Sea” in Japanese, reflects the boundless creativity and freshness that flow through our menu.

Our Story

Born from a passion for culinary artistry, Ichigo no Umi was founded with the vision of reimagining traditional Japanese dining. Strawberries, cherished in Japan as a symbol of sweetness and seasonal delight, became the heart of our concept. By weaving this beloved fruit into sushi, mochi, and innovative creations, we bring a refreshing twist to fine dining that surprises and delights.

The Ichigo Experience

Step into a space where modern elegance meets Japanese minimalism. Our interiors are designed to transport you. Subtle lighting, refined details, and an intimate atmosphere enhance every bite. Whether for a romantic evening, a celebration, or a quiet escape, Ichigo no Umi offers more than a meal — it’s a journey of the senses.

At Ichigo no Umi, we celebrate more than food — we celebrate culture. From limited-edition menus during Japan’s strawberry blossom season to curated sake-pairing nights, our experiences are designed to immerse guests in a world where tradition and innovation meet.
